assets/falling-blocks.nes is Legally Distinct Falling Block Game by Zorchenhimer
(MIT, see Falling-Blocks.txt). Built from e64ef29 with ca65/ld65 V2.19.
The first 16 bytes use an equivalent iNES 1.0 header for QuickNES: mapper 5,
64 KiB PRG, 16 KiB CHR, vertical mirroring and 8 KiB battery-backed PRG RAM.
PRG and CHR data are unchanged from that build. The audio driver is FamiStudio
by Mathieu Gauthier.
https://github.com/zorchenhimer/nes-tetris/tree/e64ef29e4b68decd3f2c36a8f9f3cd7d679a4b7c

assets/mines.nes is NES-Minesweeper (MIT, see NES-Minesweeper.txt).
Built from the unmodified source using ca65 and ld65 V2.19:
ca65 src/main.asm -g -o build/main.o
ld65 -o mines.nes -C src/linker.cfg build/main.o
https://github.com/ProgrEmily/NES-Minesweeper/tree/1c376724227f4707147af8c7f496898d22fcc2c4

assets/nes.wasm uses Nes_Emu by Shay Green and contributors to libretro's
QuickNES_Core. The original core is LGPL-2.1-or-later and includes additional
GPL-2.0-or-later mappers. The module and its bridge are distributed under
GPL-2.0-or-later. See QuickNES-GPL-2.0.txt and QuickNES-LGPL-2.1.txt.
The VRC7 synthesizer incorporates emu2413 by Mitsutaka Okazaki.
Original notices and the complete core sources are in core/quicknes-src.tar.gz.
core/build.sh and core/bridge.cpp reproduce the module with Emscripten 4.0.4.
https://github.com/libretro/QuickNES_Core/tree/26bb785c9deddb66a17717b21bb4e328f03ade32
