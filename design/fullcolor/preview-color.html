06 / GUIDELINES

Leave room for the world.

Do

  • Show one clear result and keep essential actions reachable.
  • Use white text, bright accents, and transparent containers.
  • Keep text at 14–24 dp and icons at least 12 × 12.
  • Keep the outermost card borderless; use 1px / 30% white internal borders.
  • Keep text, icons, and other foreground alpha at or above 40%.
  • Pair color with text, icons, or shapes.
  • Check real backgrounds and interactions on the target glasses.

Don't

  • Shrink typography to fit more content.
  • Add large solid backgrounds or use dark critical details.
  • Expect black to hide the physical environment.
  • Dim a parent card and weaken all its children together.
  • Clip essential content or reduce required spacing to fit.
  • Treat a desktop preview as proof of optical readability.
